How the page grew

Twenty-four posts. No ads. Just editing that people watched to the end.

I started an Instagram page and made one Reel a day, showing a project I was working on. I shot, edited and captioned all of them myself, mostly on my phone with CapCut and VN.

I kept the first second tight, cut on the beat and put clean captions on everything, because most people watch with the sound off. I made the covers and post designs in Canva so the grid looked consistent. When I got stuck on a hook or a caption, I brainstormed with ChatGPT.
